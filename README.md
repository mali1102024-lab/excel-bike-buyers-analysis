# Bike Buyers Dashboard (Microsoft Excel Project)

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes bike purchase behavior using demographic and lifestyle data.  
The dataset includes customer details such as marital status, gender, income, education, occupation, commute distance, region, age, and whether they purchased a bike.

## 📊 Dataset
- Source: Excel dataset (`bike_buyers`)
- Columns: ID, Marital Status, Gender, Income, Children, Education, Occupation, Home Owner, Cars, Commute Distance, Region, Age, Purchased Bike
- Rows: ~1,000+ customer records

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Income vs Purchase**: Comparison of average income by gender and purchase status
- **Age Brackets**: Bike purchase counts by age group
- **Commute Distance**: Purchase behavior by commute distance
- **Filters**: Marital Status, Region, Education

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning in Excel
- Interactive Filters, Parameters, and Calculated Fields
- Dashboard & Storyboard features

## 🎯 Key Insights
- Middle‑aged customers are more likely to purchase bikes.
- Short commute distances (0–1 miles) show higher purchase rates.
- Female non‑buyers had higher average incomes compared to buyers.

## 🎯 Storytelling
Our analysis of bike buyers shows that middle-aged customers are the most likely to purchase bikes, especially those with short commutes under 1 mile. Interestingly, higher income does not guarantee purchase — in fact, many high-income females did not buy bikes. This suggests that lifestyle and convenience, rather than income, drive bike purchase decisions.

Based on these insights, marketing should focus on middle-aged professionals with short commutes, positioning bikes as a practical and efficient commuting option.
